# Broadcast acceptance plan

These numerical limits are proposed pass/fail thresholds, not measurements. The owner and providers must approve or revise them before the test begins. Record immutable release and configuration fingerprints, input frame rate, approved assets, clock accuracy, receiver implementation and sampling intervals. Use independent receivers for each declared output, plus transport timestamps, reference audio markers and synchronized logs.

Use controlled fault injection and keep recovery windows distinct from routine transitions and endurance. Close release only when security, recovery and playback evidence are accepted. See [update and rollback](UPDATE_AND_ROLLBACK_PLAN.md).

## Phase Six Measured Local Acceptance Evidence vs. Open 72-Hour Staging Gate

In Phase Six, the automated two-channel Full HD acceptance fixture ([two-channel-playout-acceptance.js](../backend/verification/two-channel-playout-acceptance.js)) verified:

- **Simultaneous Two-Channel Full HD 1920أ—1080 H.264/AAC Output**: Independent `ffprobe` and `ffmpeg` UDP receivers confirmed concurrent `1920أ—1080 @ 25 fps` H.264 video and `48 kHz` stereo AAC audio on Channel One (`UDP 26310`) and Channel Two (`UDP 26312`).
- **Channel One Camera Rotation & Independent Audio**: Automatic sequential rotation between live 1080p camera feeds with `1.2 ms`â€“`4.8 ms` pad-switch latency, zero encoder restarts, continuous independent `440 Hz` music audio while camera audio remained muted, automatic `FALLBACK_ACTIVE` transition when all cameras were excluded, and automatic recovery to `RUNNING` when cameras returned.
- **Channel Two 2+ Hour Full HD Movie Playout, Ad Insertion & Safe Seeking**: Generated, uploaded (via resumable chunked upload with SHA-256 verification), and validated a **2-hour-and-5-minute (`7,500.021`-second / 125-minute)** `1920أ—1080` MP4 movie, verified automatic transition from an intro clip and advertisement break into the 2h05m feature film, and verified keyframe seeking to the `+3,600,000 ms` (1-hour) mark without loading the movie into memory.
- **Cross-Channel Fault Isolation & Worker Crash Recovery**: Killed Channel One's GStreamer worker with `SIGKILL`, verified Channel Two continued broadcasting with `0` restarts, and verified Channel One automatically respawned its worker (`restartCount: 1`) and returned to `RUNNING` within `~2.1 seconds`.
- **Open Production Endurance Gates**: The 30-minute, 2-hour wall-clock, and mandatory **72-hour continuous two-channel Full HD soak test** as well as **10+ GB file upload endurance** remain open until executed on the dedicated Linux/MySQL staging server. See [PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md](PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md).
